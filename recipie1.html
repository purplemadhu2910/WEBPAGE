BANANA CUPCAKES

In this recipie you can try making banana cupcakes from scratch that too eggless....Join with me and enjoyy baking!!

INGREDIENTS

    • Refines Floue - 1 cup
    • Milk powder – ½ cup
    • Powdered sugar – ½ cup
    • Ripe banana – 2 small
    • Baking powder – 1 tsp
    • White/unsalted butter – 50 gm
    • Vanilla essence – ½ tsp

METHOD:

      Pre- heat oven at 250° C for 10 mins.
      In a bowl soften the butter. Add powdered sugar and beat till white, glossy and soft.
      In another bowl mix all the dry ingredients and mix properly.
      Add the flour mixture spoon by spoon to the butter mixture. Mix properly.
      The batter will be somewhat thick after all the flour is added.
      Now in a bowl smash the bananas. Add this to the batter and mix. Bananas will give the required consistency to the batter. If still thick add little milk and mix.
      Line the cupcake tray with paper cups.
      Pour the batter into the paper cups to ¾ level, leaving space for the cupcake to rise.
      Bake at 180° C for 20 to 25 mins.
      Now take out from oven and cool it and enjoyyyy
